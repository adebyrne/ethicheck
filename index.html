My checks

Two signals are checked separately: the declared origin on the product record, and the GS1 barcode registry (first digits of the barcode). The registry shows where the company registered its barcode — companies manufacture worldwide, so it's a hint, not proof of origin.

In the full app, brand and campaign lists are crowdsourced with citations and voting. In this prototype they're personal and saved on your device.
