Things to avoid

"Prefer certified" adds a green line when a product carries Fairtrade, Rainforest Alliance, MSC, RSPO, UTZ, organic or B Corp certification. It never marks a product bad for lacking one — plenty of good products aren't certified.

Countries to avoid

Two signals, checked separately: the declared origin on the product record, and the GS1 barcode registry (the first digits of the barcode). The registry shows where the company registered its barcode — companies manufacture worldwide, so treat it as a hint, not proof.

Brands to block

Accents are ignored, so "nestle" matches "Nestlé".

Where you shop

Used to keep suggested alternatives to products sold near you. Leave empty to search worldwide.
